# Coach Perfect — Meredith's Week 1 Launch Playbook

## BLUF
Get Meredith using Coach Perfect with 3 real clients in 7 days. This is the single most important thing right now. Every minute of planning has been done. Time to execute.

---

## DAY 1 (Monday): SETUP — 45 minutes

### You (Ned) — Before Meredith Touches It
- [ ] Deploy the app to a live URL (Vercel/Railway — takes 10 min)
- [ ] Create Meredith's coach account (email + password)
- [ ] Set her plan to Professional (no billing yet — just flip the flag)
- [ ] Verify the diagnostic flow works end-to-end on mobile
- [ ] Test: Can she add a client? Send a diagnostic? Log a session?

### Meredith — Guided Walkthrough (30 min call)
- [ ] Walk her through the dashboard on her laptop
- [ ] Show her the mobile app (scan QR code → install to home screen)
- [ ] Demo: Add a test client → Send diagnostic → See results appear
- [ ] Demo: Log a session note → Add tasks → See it in the task list
- [ ] Answer questions. Write down every piece of friction.
- [ ] **Ask her:** "Which 3 clients would benefit most from this?"

**Exit criteria:** Meredith can independently add a client and send a diagnostic.

---

## DAY 2 (Tuesday): FIRST REAL CLIENT — 20 minutes

### Meredith's Action
- [ ] Pick Client #1 — ideally someone she has a session with this week
- [ ] Add them to Coach Perfect (name, email, company, industry)
- [ ] Send the diagnostic invite email
- [ ] Text the client: "Hey, I'm using a new tool to track our progress. Can you take a 10-min assessment? Check your email."

### Your Job
- [ ] Monitor: Did the diagnostic invite send? Did the client open it?
- [ ] Be on standby for any Meredith questions (Slack/text)
- [ ] Fix any bugs immediately

**Exit criteria:** Client #1 has received and opened the diagnostic invite.

---

## DAY 3 (Wednesday): CLIENT COMPLETES DIAGNOSTIC

### What Should Happen
- [ ] Client completes 48 questions (~10 min)
- [ ] Business Health Score generates instantly
- [ ] Meredith gets the "Diagnostic Results" email with score breakdown
- [ ] Score appears on Meredith's dashboard
- [ ] Radar chart and category breakdown populate

### Meredith's Reaction (capture this)
- [ ] "What was your first reaction when you saw their score?"
- [ ] "Was anything surprising?"
- [ ] "How will this change your next session with them?"

### If Client Hasn't Completed Yet
- [ ] Meredith sends a gentle nudge: "Did you get a chance to do that assessment? It'll make our next session really productive."
- [ ] You check: Is the link working? Any technical issues?

**Exit criteria:** At least 1 client has a completed diagnostic with BHS score.

---

## DAY 4-5 (Thu-Fri): FIRST COACHED SESSION WITH DATA

### Before the Session
- [ ] Meredith reviews the AI Session Prep brief
- [ ] She looks at: Overall BHS, weakest category, strongest category
- [ ] She plans her opening: "I had you take an assessment. Let me show you what it says about your business..."

### During the Session
- [ ] Meredith shares the BHS with the client (screen share or in-person on tablet)
- [ ] Walk through the radar chart: "Here's where you're strong, here's where there's opportunity"
- [ ] Focus on the weakest category: "Let's talk about [Financial/Operations/etc.]"
- [ ] Assign 2-3 tasks in Coach Perfect (client sees them in their view)
- [ ] Log session notes in Coach Perfect immediately after

### After the Session
- [ ] Meredith logs the session in the app
- [ ] She captures at least 1 "win" from the conversation
- [ ] She assigns 2-3 follow-up tasks with due dates

### Debrief with Ned (15 min call)
- [ ] "How did showing the diagnostic change the conversation?"
- [ ] "Did the client react differently than a normal session?"
- [ ] "What was clunky? What needs fixing?"
- [ ] Record her answers — this is testimonial material

**Exit criteria:** 1 real coaching session completed using Coach Perfect data.

---

## DAY 6-7 (Weekend): CLIENTS #2 AND #3

### Meredith's Action
- [ ] Add Client #2 and Client #3
- [ ] Send diagnostic invites to both
- [ ] Follow up with text nudges if not completed by Sunday night

### Your Job

## WHAT MEREDITH SHOULD SAY TO CLIENTS

### Intro Script (via text or email before diagnostic)
> "Hey [name], I'm rolling out a new tool that measures your business health across 8 categories. It takes about 10 minutes and gives you an instant score with recommendations. I think it'll make our sessions way more focused. Can you take it before our next meeting? [link]"

### In-Session Script (showing results)
> "So I had you take that assessment — here's what it found. Your overall Business Health Score is [X] out of 100. You're strongest in [category] which makes sense given [reason]. The area with the most room to grow is [category] — let's dig into that today."

### Follow-Up Script (after session)
> "Great session today. I've added your action items to the platform so we can track them together. You'll see them in your dashboard. I'll check in next week with a quick pulse survey — takes 30 seconds."

---

## WHAT TO WATCH FOR (RED FLAGS)

| Signal | What It Means | Action |
|--------|--------------|--------|
| Client doesn't complete diagnostic | Link might be broken, or not compelling enough | Check deliverability. Add a text nudge. |
| Meredith doesn't log session notes | Too many steps, or doesn't see the value | Simplify. Reduce to 1 required field. |
| Client says "this is cool" but doesn't engage with tasks | Task view might be confusing | Watch their first task interaction. Simplify UI. |
| Meredith says "I don't have time" | Onboarding is too heavy | Cut scope. Focus on diagnostic-only for week 1. |
| Diagnostic takes >15 min | Questions are too confusing | Watch completion drop-off rates. Rewrite unclear questions. |
