## MEETING SCRIPT

### Pre-Meeting Prep
- Review Meredith's website, services, RISE program, team
- Prepare demo dashboard (mock data showing coaching KPIs)
- Print or share pitch deck digitally
- Have pricing sheet ready
- Know her testimonials and reference them

### Opening (5 minutes)
```
"Meredith, thank you for making time today. I've been studying your practice
and I'm genuinely impressed by what you've built — the RISE program, the CEO
Roundtable, the breadth of services. I can see why leaders like Chris
Ciesielski and Delaine Henry speak so highly of you.

I want to share something I've been building that I believe could be a
game-changer for your practice — and a significant revenue opportunity. It
won't add to your workload; it'll cut it in half. Let me show you what I mean."
```

### Pain Discovery (10 minutes)
```
"Before I dive in, I'd love to understand a few things:

1. When you think about scaling your practice, what's the biggest bottleneck?
2. How do you currently track client progress between sessions?
3. How much time per week would you estimate goes to admin — scheduling,
   notes, follow-ups, organizing materials?
4. When a CEO asks you 'how do I know coaching is working?' — what do you
   show them?
5. If you could snap your fingers and fix one thing about how your practice
   operates, what would it be?"
```

### Demo & Solution (15 minutes)
```
"Let me show you Coach Perfect. [Open dashboard demo]

This is what your practice could look like. [Walk through coach dashboard]
- Here are all your clients at a glance — green means on track, yellow needs
  attention, red is overdue.
- Click any client and you see their diagnostic results, session history,
  action items, documents, and KPIs.
- This diagnostic engine? A new client fills out a 10-minute questionnaire
  and Coach Perfect automatically generates a gap analysis across finance,
  operations, people, strategy, culture, and growth.
- Watch this: I click 'Generate Quarterly Review' and in 30 seconds, it
  builds a branded slide deck with your logo, pulling real data from the
  client's dashboard.

Now here's the client view. [Switch to client dashboard]
- Your clients log in and see THEIR business KPIs, THEIR action items,
  THEIR progress over time.
- This is what you show a CEO when they ask 'is coaching working?'

And the automation. [Show workflow]
- When you add a new client, this entire onboarding sequence fires
  automatically. Welcome email, diagnostic questionnaire, coaching agreement,
  calendar invite — you don't touch any of it.
- After every session, action items get emailed to the client with deadlines.
  If they miss a deadline, they get a nudge. If they're silent for 30 days,
  you get an alert."
```

### Revenue Opportunity (10 minutes)
```
"Here's where it gets interesting for your business.

Option 1: You bundle Coach Perfect into your coaching packages. You're already
charging for coaching — now you add $100-200/month for 'digital coaching
platform access.' Clients see it as premium value. With 20 clients, that's
$24,000-48,000 in additional annual revenue.

Option 2: You offer Business Diagnostic Audits as a standalone service.
$2,500-5,000 per audit. The platform does the analysis; you deliver the
insights. That's a service you can offer to companies who aren't even
coaching clients yet.

Option 3: You white-label the entire platform. 'Meredith Eicher Business
Intelligence.' Your brand, your URL, your clients. Every business leader
in your network becomes a potential subscriber.

And passive income: every person you refer to Coach Perfect earns you 10-30%
recurring commission. Your CEO Roundtable members, Peer Group participants,
RISE graduates — that's 200+ potential users."
```

```
No cost. No risk. Just a chance to see if this is the tool that takes your
practice to the next level.

What's the best next step for you?"
```

### Diagnostic Intake Form — Data Checklist
**Category 1: Financial Health (8 questions)**
- Current annual revenue (range)
- Revenue trend (growing/flat/declining)
- Profit margin (range)
- Cash flow status (comfortable/tight/critical)
- AR aging (% over 60 days)
- Monthly burn rate vs. revenue
- Financial reporting frequency
- Budget vs. actual tracking method

**Category 2: Operations (8 questions)**
- Number of employees
- Documented processes/SOPs (yes/no/partial)
- Technology stack maturity (manual/basic tools/integrated systems)
- Customer delivery consistency (1-10)
- Bottleneck identification (top 3)
- Quality control mechanisms
- Vendor/supplier management
- Capacity utilization estimate

**Category 3: People & Culture (8 questions)**
- Employee turnover rate (last 12 months)
- Hiring difficulty (easy/moderate/hard)
- Training/development investment (hours/year/employee)
- Team communication effectiveness (1-10)
- Culture description (3 words)
- Employee satisfaction measurement method
- Organizational structure clarity
- Succession planning status

**Category 4: Strategy & Vision (8 questions)**
- Written strategic plan (yes/no, when updated)
- 3-year revenue goal
- Competitive differentiation (top 3)
- Market position (leader/challenger/follower/niche)
- Innovation investment (% of revenue)
- Strategic planning cadence
- Board/advisory involvement
- Exit/succession timeline

**Category 5: Leadership & Growth (8 questions)**
- Leadership team size and tenure
- Decision-making speed (fast/moderate/slow)
- Delegation effectiveness (1-10)
- Personal development investment (hours/month)
- Work-life balance satisfaction (1-10)
- Biggest leadership challenge (open text)
- Growth rate target (next 12 months)
- Biggest obstacle to growth (open text)

**Category 6: Marketing & Sales (8 questions)**
- Lead generation primary channel
- Sales cycle length
- Customer acquisition cost (known/unknown/estimate)
- Customer retention rate
- Marketing budget (% of revenue)
- Digital presence maturity (none/basic/intermediate/advanced)
- Referral system (formal/informal/none)
- Brand recognition in market (strong/moderate/weak)
